## <span>1\.</span> Current use of cookies

This website does not utilize cookies of any kind, including functional, analytical, advertising, or third-party cookies. No cookie consent mechanism is provided to visitors, as none is necessary; there is nothing to consent to.

## <span>2\.</span>  Why no cookies are used

This website is intentionally built without tracking infrastructure. No visitor analytics, no advertising, and no cross-site tracking are part of how this site operates, and cookies are not required to deliver its content.

## <span>3\.</span>  Future changes

Should this change occur, such as the implementation of first-party, functional usage measurement, no tracking will be conducted without prior disclosure. The policy will be amended in advance of any such modification, and a cookie consent mechanism will be implemented if mandated by law. Additional details regarding the handling of future tracking are outlined in the Privacy Policy.
